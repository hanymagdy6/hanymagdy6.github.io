About

Hany Magdy

Graphic designer · Cairo

I am a graphic designer in Cairo with 6+ years in branding, social media (including forex and trading content) and print (event gates, backdrops and standees). I work in Photoshop, Illustrator and InDesign. My work has served events and campaigns across Dubai/UAE, Qatar, Oman, Bahrain, South Africa and Egypt.

My clients include Clarena Egypt, Essse Coffee and Smart Vision. I am a Fine Arts graduate of Minia University. I design brand identities, packaging, social media campaigns and print for clients in beauty, fitness, real estate, education and events, in Arabic and English.

Freelance graphic designer

I studied Book Arts in the Graphic Department at Minia University (2018 to 2023, Very Good with Honors). I started freelancing while still at college, then moved into in-house design work for brands in Cairo. I design identities, social media campaigns (including forex and trading content) and print such as event gates, backdrops and standees, and I use AI tools in my workflow.
